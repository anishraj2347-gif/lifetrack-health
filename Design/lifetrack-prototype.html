Where it is
On this phone
Your records, notes and wearable logs. This is the only complete copy. LifeTrack has no server that holds it.
In the backup vault
Doctor-issued documents only, encrypted before they leave. Notes and wearable logs stay here unless you turn backup on for them. Your Emergency Medical ID never goes at all.
Nowhere else, unless you tap
A cloud check sends values, units and your age — never your name, your dates, your doctor or your notes. It happens once per tap, and never on its own.
What you can do
See every access
Who opened what, and when — including this app's own reading.
Correct a record
Open any record and edit the value. Corrections are logged like every other change.
Withdraw a permission
Any grant, at any time, from Share. Withdrawing stops the next read, not the ones already made.
Erasure
This deletes your records from this phone, deletes the backup vault, and switches off every permission you have granted. It cannot be undone.
Two things survive, and you should know which before you decide.
The access log stays. It records who opened what and when, never what your results said, and it is what lets you show later that a doctor read your file — including this erasure.
